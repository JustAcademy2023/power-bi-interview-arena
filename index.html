Five levels every Power BI panel walks through

LVL 1

Power BI Basics

The warm up. Sound clear, sound curious.

What is Power BI?

A Microsoft business intelligence platform that connects to data, cleans it, models it, calculates measures and publishes interactive reports through Desktop and the online Service.

Desktop versus Service?

Desktop is where you build models and reports. The Service is where you publish, schedule refreshes, share, secure and collaborate.

Import or DirectQuery?

Import is fast and fully featured but as fresh as the last refresh. DirectQuery keeps data at the source for freshness or size, at the cost of speed.

LVL 2

Data Modelling

Where freshers and professionals separate.

Why a star schema?

One fact table surrounded by dimension tables keeps filters predictable, DAX simple and the model compact and quick.

Column or measure?

Columns are stored per row and used for slicing. Measures are calculated live from filter context and are used for aggregations.

Why a date table?

Time intelligence needs a continuous calendar marked as the date table, otherwise year to date and previous year results go wrong.

LVL 3

DAX Concepts

The language of context. Explain, do not recite.

Row context versus filter context?

Row context evaluates one row at a time. Filter context is the set of filters from slicers, visuals and other measures that decides which rows a calculation sees.

What does CALCULATE do?

It evaluates an expression after modifying the filter context, and it triggers context transition when used inside a row context.

SUM or SUMX?

SUM adds one column. SUMX iterates a table row by row, evaluates an expression for each row, then adds the results.

LVL 4

Power Query and Cloud

The unglamorous knowledge that saves reports.

Merge versus Append?

Append stacks tables vertically. Merge joins tables side by side on matching keys, like a database join.

What is query folding?

Transformation steps are pushed to the source as one native query, so the database does the work and refresh stays fast.

Why a gateway?

It lets the online Service securely reach on premises data so scheduled refresh can run.

LVL 5

Dashboards and Scenarios

Tell the story behind the visuals.

What makes a good dashboard?

It answers one question for one audience in seconds, uses restrained colour, honest chart types and interaction that rewards curiosity.

Tool choice between BI tools?

Decide by team skills, existing stack, data volume and budget, not loyalty. Naming trade offs sounds more senior than naming a winner.

Walk me through your project.

Problem, messy data, model, two or three measures you are proud of, the insight, one mistake you fixed. Keep it under three minutes.

THE DAX LAB

Five measures worth knowing by heart

Interviewers love these patterns because each one reveals how you think about context. Copy them into a practice model and change one thing at a time.

Total Sales 
Total Sales =
SUMX( Sales, Sales[Qty] * Sales[Price] )

An iterator, because quantity times price does not exist as a column.

Sales Last Year 
Sales LY =
CALCULATE( [Total Sales],
  SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR( 'Date'[Date] ) )

Needs a proper, continuous date table marked as the calendar.

Share of Total 
Share % =
DIVIDE( [Total Sales],
  CALCULATE( [Total Sales], REMOVEFILTERS( Product ) ) )

The denominator ignores product filters. DIVIDE handles zero safely.

Growth with Variables 
Growth % =
VAR Curr = [Total Sales]
VAR Prev = [Sales LY]
RETURN DIVIDE( Curr - Prev, Prev )

Variables make formulas readable, faster and easier to debug.

Top Products 
Product Rank =
RANKX( ALL( Product[Name] ),
  [Total Sales], , DESC, Dense )

ALL widens the field of competitors so the rank ignores current filters.

BOSS FIGHTS

Scenario questions that decide the offer

Panels stop asking definitions and hand you a business problem. Here is how to approach the four that appear most often.

📊

The Project Office

Build a project management dashboard: portfolio page for leaders, drillthrough for managers, budget variance, overdue tasks and row level security.

📣

The Campaign Mess

Marketing data analytics across channels: cost per lead, conversion rate, return on ad spend, and honest notes about attribution limits.

📱

The App Funnel

Product analytics from event data: active users, retention cohorts, funnel drop off and feature adoption on a compact star model.

🐌

The Slow Report

Tens of millions of rows and a sluggish page. Trim columns, aggregate, fix relationships, then measure with Performance Analyzer.

QUICK ANSWERS

Questions people ask before an interview

Short, direct answers for readers and for search assistants.

What do Power BI interview questions usually test?

Data modelling, DAX concepts, Power Query, dashboard design and your ability to explain a real Power BI project clearly and honestly.

What are the fundamental concepts of DAX?

Syntax and functions, row context, filter context and relationships between tables. CALCULATE ties them together by changing filter context.

How do I crack a Power BI interview?

Build a small project from import to publishing, break it on purpose, fix it, and explain each decision aloud, ideally to a mentor who interrupts.
